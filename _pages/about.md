Our group has a broad interest in **polymerization catalysis**, with a focus on **sustainability**. 

We aim to make “better polymers” by **developing new catalytic systems** able, for example, to polymerize underexplored monomers, or to perform precise synthesis of well-defined (co)polymers.  

Thus, our core expertise is in catalysis applied to polymer chemistry, including **synthesis of ligands and metal complexes** (early-TM, main group elements), application to **ring-opening polymerization** (ROP) and copolymerization (ROCOP), **mechanistic studies using DFT** as well as basic polymer characterization. 

Our projects span all along the life cycle of plastics, from designing new monomers, investigating new catalysts as well as looking at polymers end-of-life. 

For more details, don’t hesitate to look at our ongoing projects or to contact us. We are always happy to collaborate.
