Marianna is a PhD student part of the Science and Solutions for a Changing Planet Doctoral Training Partnership (SSCP DTP). 

She received her MChem degree from the University of Warwick, in which she did her final year project under supervision of Professor Martin Wills working on the asymmetric transfer hydrogenation of diketones. 

Her PhD project focuses on polymer design, working towards enhancing the biodegradation of polylactic acid.

Outside of the lab, Marianna loves to watch movies, travel, and explore new restaurants.
